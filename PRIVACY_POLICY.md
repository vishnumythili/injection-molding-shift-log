# Privacy Policy — Shift Log

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

Shift Log ("the app") is a production-logging tool for manufacturing shifts.

**Data we collect:** none. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or
production data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access.

**Where your data lives:** everything you enter (dates, shifts, machines, products, quantities,
rejections, downtime, operator names, remarks) is stored only in a local database on your device.
If Android device backup is enabled on your phone, Google may include the app's data in your
device backup under Google's own privacy terms.

**Exporting:** when you tap Export, the app creates a CSV file and hands it to the app you choose
(email, Drive, WhatsApp, etc.). What happens after that is governed by that app.

**Children:** the app is intended for workplace use and is not directed at children.

**Deleting your data:** delete an entry inside the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall it
in Android settings to remove everything.
