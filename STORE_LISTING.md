# Play Store listing text

**App name (≤30):** Shift Log: Production Tracker

**Short description (≤80):**
Log shift production, rejections and downtime in seconds. Works offline.

**Full description (≤4000):**
Shift Log is a fast, no-nonsense production tracker for injection moulding and other manufacturing shops.

Record each shift in under a minute:
• Date, shift (A/B/C), machine and product
• Target vs produced quantity
• Rejections by reason — flash, short shot, sink marks, burn marks, black spots, flow marks, warpage, dimension, other
• Downtime minutes and reason
• Operator and remarks

See the picture instantly:
• Live good / rejected / rejection % while you type
• Today's totals on the home screen
• Reports for today, 7 days, 30 days or all time
• Rejections by reason, results by machine, downtime by reason
• Export to CSV and open in Excel or Google Sheets

Private and simple:
• Works fully offline
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your data stays on your phone
